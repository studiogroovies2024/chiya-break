How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above. If Chrome asks "Download anyway?", tap Download.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open (or find ChiyaBreak-0.24.0-tester.apk in Files → Downloads).
  3. If you see "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says "App not scanned" or "Unsafe app blocked", tap More details → Install anyway. This only happens because the app is not on the Play Store yet.
  5. Open Chiya Break from your home screen and enjoy a chiya break!

Good to know

  • After installing, you can turn Allow from this source off again (Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps).
  • When Chiya Break comes to Google Play, uninstall this tester version first, then install from Play. Your scores and coins on this tester version won't carry over.
  • No account and no sign-up. Your progress stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
