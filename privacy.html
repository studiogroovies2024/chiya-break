Chiya Break — Privacy Policy

Groovies Production Studio · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Short version: Chiya Break has no accounts and we do not collect your personal information. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

Who we are

Chiya Break is a mobile game made by Groovies Production Studio (Nepal). In this policy, "we" means Groovies Production Studio and "the game" means the Chiya Break app.

Information stored on your phone

The game saves your best scores, coins, unlocked themes and settings (sound, vibration) only on your own device. This information is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. There is no sign-in, no account, and the game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (banner ads, full-screen ads between games, and optional "watch an ad" rewards). To show ads, prevent fraud and measure results, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device type, operating system, language and app usage related to ads (for example, which ads were shown or tapped)

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Europe, UK and other regions where the law requires it: the game shows Google's consent message when you first open it, so you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can turn off personalised ads for all apps in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or "Delete advertising ID").
  • If the game cannot get a consent answer, it asks Google for non-personalised ads only.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not store any on our servers. Information handled by Google AdMob is protected under Google's own security practices.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
